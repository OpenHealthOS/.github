# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenHealthOS is committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community
for everyone. We expect contributors, maintainers, and participants to act
professionally and with empathy, especially when discussing healthcare,
research, and AI.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing backgrounds, experiences, and expertise.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus discussion on ideas, evidence, and the project.
- Protect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive or identifiable health data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, sexualized
content, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not tolerated. Neither is
sharing confidential, personally identifiable, or protected health
information without authorization.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other appropriate
action in response to violations. Report concerns privately through the
organization's security reporting channel or by contacting a maintainer. Do
not include sensitive details in a public issue.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
