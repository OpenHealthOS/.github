# OpenHealthOS

### Open-source infrastructure for the next generation of healthcare technology.

OpenHealthOS is an open-source, cloud-native healthcare technology platform
for building interoperable healthcare applications, clinical data systems,
and AI-powered healthcare solutions.

Our goal is simple:

> **Make healthcare technology more open, interoperable, extensible, and
> accessible to developers, researchers, and innovators.**

---

## 🧬 What We're Building

OpenHealthOS brings together healthcare interoperability, modern cloud
architecture, and artificial intelligence into an extensible open-source
platform.
